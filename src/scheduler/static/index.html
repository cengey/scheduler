How Rotations Work in the Scheduler

Each rotation defines a teacher's schedule pattern repeating every 2 weeks:

  • Fixed Weekday Off: Teachers never get scheduled on their designated off day (e.g. Group 1 = Thursday, Group 2 = Friday, Group 3 = Tuesday, Group 4 = Wednesday).
  • Alternate Weekends: In Week 1, Saturday and Sunday are marked OFF. In Week 2, Saturday and Sunday are Shifted (working).
  • Repeats from Week 1: The rotation continuously alternates between 4 working days (Week 1) and 6 working days (Week 2). Maximum 6 consecutive days is strictly maintained.
