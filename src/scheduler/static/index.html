Alternate Weekend Rotation Logic

Teachers are assigned to either Weekend Group A or Weekend Group B. The scheduler enforces alternating weekends based on the calendar week number:

  • Even Calendar Weeks: Group A is OFF; Group B works Saturday & Sunday.
  • Odd Calendar Weeks: Group B is OFF; Group A works Saturday & Sunday.
  • Weekend status is strictly calendar-based and does not change if a teacher takes ad-hoc leave or sick days.
